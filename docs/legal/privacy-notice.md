[School / Clinic legal name] operates CLINiQ for School clinic services. This notice explains how we process personal information through the clinic system, patient portal, and emergency health passport service. It is written for the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules.

## 1. Information we process

Depending on the service you use and your role, CLINiQ processes:

- identity and School information: name, School ID number, date of birth, sex, patient category, program, department, year/section, enrollment or employment details, and profile photo;
- account and device information: email address, password hash, account status, remembered-device information, login time, and security records such as hashed failed-login identifiers and IP addresses;
- contact information: your contact details and the name, relationship, and contact details of emergency contacts or guardians;
- health and clinic information: blood type, allergies, conditions, medications, height, weight, BMI, vital signs, concerns, symptoms, assessment, diagnosis, treatment, referrals, clinic visit notes, APE findings and status, and supporting APE documents;
- service information: appointment requests, clinic notifications, medicine dispensing, equipment loans, referral status, and visit-linked feedback;
- emergency and safety information: emergency-passport token status, selected emergency information, passport-access records, incident-report details, reporter details, location, optional photo, IP address, user agent, triage/risk information, and clinic response; and
- accountability information: user role, relevant actions, date/time, IP address, user agent, and audit records.

Health information is sensitive personal information. We request only information that is relevant to the stated clinic purpose.

## 2. Why we use the information

We process information to:

- identify patients and manage authorized accounts;
- provide, document, coordinate, and follow up School clinic services and APE requirements;
- receive, review, schedule, change, or cancel appointment requests;
- maintain accurate health, visit, referral, medicine, and equipment records;
- notify patients about their clinic account, appointments, APE, and other clinic matters;
- support emergency response through the optional emergency passport and incident-report workflow;
- maintain service quality through visit-linked feedback and aggregated reporting;
- secure the Service, prevent misuse, investigate incidents, enforce access controls, and maintain audit trails; and
- meet legal, regulatory, safety, and records-management obligations.

We rely on consent where required, including specific consent for processing sensitive personal information when no other lawful basis applies. We may also process information when necessary to provide clinic services, fulfill legal duties, protect life or health, or pursue legitimate security and operational interests, subject to applicable law. Where a particular collection or disclosure needs consent, we will present a separate, clear consent request at that point.

## 3. Emergency passport: important limited disclosure

The emergency passport is optional. If you enable it, a person with the QR/NFC link may be able to view the emergency information selected for your passport in order to help during a genuine or suspected emergency. The displayed information can include your name, photo, School ID number, course/department, blood type, allergies, conditions, medications, emergency instructions, and emergency-contact details; BMI is displayed only if you allow it in the passport setting.

Opening the passport creates an access record, including time, IP address, user agent, and, when a viewer signs in, the viewer's identity. A viewer may submit an incident report to the clinic. You can ask the clinic to disable your passport token; disabling it prevents new passport access but does not erase records already created for a prior emergency or security event.

## 4. Who may receive information

Access is role-based. Authorized clinic staff and administrators may access information necessary for their assigned work. Patients may access their own portal information. Authorized personnel may receive reports or records when required for School-clinic operations, safety, lawful referral, or legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not disclose health information to third parties for advertising. We may disclose information to a health provider, emergency responder, government authority, insurer, or other recipient only when you authorize it, when needed to protect life or health, when required or permitted by law, or for a documented clinic referral or service purpose. Any service provider that processes personal information for us must be appropriately authorized and bound to protect it.

The system uses account authentication, role-based access, password hashing, security logs, session protections, access logging, and technical safeguards intended to reduce unauthorized access. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure; please protect your credentials and report suspected account misuse promptly.

## 6. Your data privacy rights

Subject to legal limits and applicable procedures, you may request to be informed about, access, correct, object to, erase or block, obtain a portable copy of, or seek damages concerning your personal information. You may withdraw consent for a consent-based activity; this does not affect processing already carried out lawfully and may limit a feature that depends on that information. You may also lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

To make a request, contact the DPO/privacy contact above. Please state your name, School ID number, request, and preferred reply method. We may request information to verify your identity and will explain if a request cannot be fully granted because of law, safety, another person's rights, or a legitimate records-retention requirement.

## 7. Children, changes, and questions

Where a patient cannot lawfully provide consent, the School will obtain consent from a parent, guardian, or other authorized representative, as applicable. The School may update this notice if our processing changes. For a material change, we will post the new date and provide an appropriate notice before or when the change takes effect.

For privacy concerns, suspected unauthorized access, or a personal-information request, contact the DPO/privacy contact above. You may also contact the National Privacy Commission through its official channels.
